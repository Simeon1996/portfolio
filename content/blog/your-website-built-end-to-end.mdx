# Your Website, Built End to End: From First Conversation to Live, Monitored, and Supported

There are three usual ways to get a website, and each one quietly hands you a second job.

The DIY site builder makes you the designer, and it shows — the result looks like everyone else's. The agency makes you the client of a process with meetings, handoffs, and an invoice to match. The marketplace freelancer builds the thing and vanishes, and six months later the domain lapses or the site goes down and there's nobody to call.

What all three have in common: you end up managing the project — chasing the designer, deciphering hosting dashboards, googling what a DNS record is at midnight. I offer the opposite arrangement. You describe what you need. I handle every step between that conversation and a live, healthy website — and I stay on call after it ships.

## The Work Speaks First

I've built and shipped websites for very different purposes and very different owners — full products with real users, content platforms, professional portfolios, AI-powered web apps and dashboards. Some of them can already be seen here, with links and screenshots collected on [the projects page](/projects) — browse it if you want to see the range before we talk. The range is the point: a product, a blog, a portfolio, and a dashboard are different jobs, and I've shipped all of them.

Most projects don't need to start from zero, and starting from zero is where budgets and timelines go to die. So I built **[The Template Studio](https://web-templates-eight.vercel.app/)** — a catalogue of **30 complete, multi-page templates**: 23 website designs and 7 native Android app designs, each with its own typography, colour palette, and temperament.

They cover most of the businesses that walk through my door: restaurants and boutique hotels, law firms and dental clinics, salons, fitness studios, real estate agencies, photography portfolios, e-commerce shops, SaaS platforms, online course platforms, nonprofits, podcasts, editorial blogs, and more. Every template opens a **live demo you can click through end to end** — real pages, real navigation, not a static thumbnail.

Choosing one doesn't mean getting a copy of it. The template is the starting world; I then rebuild it around you — your brand, your colours, your content, your pages. And if none of the thirty fits, you can **request an entirely new design**, built from scratch the same way the thirty were.

There's also a sister catalogue for a very specific occasion: [The Celebration Studio](https://celebrations-8e3r.vercel.app/) — complete wedding and christening websites with schedules, maps, RSVP, and matching print-ready invitation cards.

## "Everything Included" Means Everything

This is the part that separates this arrangement from buying a template or hiring a coder. The deliverable isn't code. It's a website that exists on the internet, stays up, and has someone responsible for it. Concretely:

- **The discussions** — we start by talking about what the site is for, who visits it, and what it has to do. You don't need to know any technical vocabulary; translating your goals into technical decisions is my job, not yours.
- **The development** — design, build, and review rounds until it's right. You see progress on a live preview link throughout, not at a big reveal at the end.
- **The domain** — I find, buy, and configure the domain for you, or wire up one you already own. You never touch a DNS record.
- **The hosting** — I deploy on whatever platform fits the project and the budget: Vercel for most modern sites, AWS or another cloud when the backend calls for it, or your own infrastructure if you have one. You're not locked into anything.
- **The monitoring** — after launch, the site's health is watched: uptime, performance, SSL certificates, broken links. If something degrades, I know before you do — and usually before your visitors do.
- **The support** — when something breaks, or you need a new page, a content change, or a feature next quarter, you message the person who built it. No ticket queue, no stranger reading the code for the first time.

One conversation, one person responsible, zero dashboards for you to learn.

## What an Engagement Looks Like

1. **We talk.** You tell me about your business and what the website needs to do.
2. **You choose a direction.** Pick a template from [the Studio](https://web-templates-eight.vercel.app/) as the starting point, or commission a fully custom design.
3. **I build, you review.** You follow along on a live preview link and we iterate until it's yours.
4. **It goes live.** Domain bought and connected, hosting set up, site launched.
5. **It stays healthy.** Monitoring runs continuously, and I'm one message away when you need changes or something goes wrong.
